* ClaudeCode Opus5.5
"Apparently, there was once a profession called coder."
In 2026, AI made that profession disappear.
Where do craftspeople go when their craft is gone?
What is human creativity?
A former coder made these clocks with AI* alone, without writing any code.
Taking "the clock" as a point of reference, this work reflects on creativity in the age of AI.
30 years after John Maeda's 12 o'clocks.
A tribute to coding culture.
The last clock work.
